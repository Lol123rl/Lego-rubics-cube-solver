# Prime Cuber

Prime Cuber is a LEGO SPIKE Prime robot that solves a Rubik's Cube.

## What it does

Prime Cuber uses LEGO SPIKE Prime motors and sensors to move a Rubik's Cube and solve it. I built the robot using LEGO pieces from the SPIKE Prime set.

The robot is made to hold the Rubik's Cube and move the different sides so it can solve the cube.

## Parts

- 1 LEGO SPIKE Prime Hub
- LEGO SPIKE Prime motors
- LEGO SPIKE Prime sensors
- LEGO Technic pieces
- LEGO SPIKE Prime cables
- 1 Rubik's Cube

## How it works

First, I put the scrambled Rubik's Cube into the robot.

The SPIKE Prime Hub controls the motors. The motors move the mechanisms that turn the Rubik's Cube.

The robot then goes through the moves needed to solve the cube.

## Programming

I programmed Prime Cuber using LEGO SPIKE Prime.

The program controls the motors and sensors and tells the robot when to move.

## Building

I built Prime Cuber using LEGO Technic pieces and parts from the LEGO SPIKE Prime set.

I designed the robot so it can hold the Rubik's Cube and turn it without the cube falling out.

## BOM

The parts I used are listed in [BOM.csv](BOM.csv).

## Wiring

The motors and sensors connect directly to the LEGO SPIKE Prime Hub using the cables that came with the SPIKE Prime set.

## Goal

My goal with Prime Cuber is to make a LEGO robot that can solve a Rubik's Cube by itself.

I also wanted to learn more about building mechanisms, programming motors, and using sensors with LEGO SPIKE Prime.
